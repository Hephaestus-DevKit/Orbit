# CUMCM 2026 compliance profile

Checked against official pages on 2026-08-05. If the organizer publishes a
newer rule or the project supplies its own rule file, the newer supplied source
wins and `paper/contest-profile.json` must record the change.

## Paper and support material

- A4 paper; all margins at least 2.5 cm.
- The electronic first page contains the title, abstract, and keywords; no
  table of contents.
- Main body is at most 30 pages and the paper PDF at most 20 MB.
- Support material is ZIP/RAR at most 20 MB, contains a file list, and includes
  complete runnable source code when code is used.
- Do not expose team identity, school, instructor, or other identifying data in
  paper or support material.

Source: <https://www.mcm.edu.cn/html_cn/node/4cd596519c9eb9fbd866398f6df0caa3.html>

## AI tools — rules effective 2026-09-01

The rule covers large-language models, generative AI, code assistants, and AI
agents. Core modeling and analysis must be team-led, and all AI output used in
the work must be manually reviewed.

Immediately before references, include an `AI工具使用声明`:

- If used: `本参赛队在竞赛过程中使用了AI工具，主要用于〖简要用途，如语言润色、代码调试等〗，详细使用情况见支撑材料。`
- If unused: use the official unused declaration. Do not choose this path if
  this Skill or another AI system contributed to the submitted work.

When AI was used, support material must include `AI工具使用详情.pdf` stating:

1. tool name, version, and model;
2. purpose and stage;
3. main prompting method and process (typical examples may be included);
4. adopted content, manual changes, and verification (language polishing is
   excepted from the detailed adoption/verification description).

Concealment, false declarations, unreviewed AI output, or handing core work to
AI can affect or cancel awards.

Source: <https://www.mcm.edu.cn/html_cn/node/fef94648f2836ab6cc81586f4c38512b.html>

## Interpretation guardrails

- AI is auxiliary; the team remains responsible for originality, legality,
  truthfulness, and accuracy.
- Do not discuss a live problem with outsiders or on public platforms.
- The frequently repeated “AIGC/AIDC below 20%” suggestion is not present in
  the official 2026 AI-use rule above. It may be a risk-management heuristic,
  but must not be encoded or reported as an official threshold unless a newer
  primary rule explicitly adds it.
